📚 Course Structure

The AI – Business Analyst programme is designed to provide practical knowledge of Business Analysis, Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics and modern digital tools.

📊 Business Analysis Fundamentals

Introduction to Business Analysis, business processes, problem identification, stakeholder analysis and business decision-making.

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence, AI applications in business, AI-assisted decision making and emerging AI technologies.

🗄️ SQL & Data Management

Database concepts, SQL fundamentals, querying data, filtering, sorting, aggregation and extracting useful business information.

📈 Advanced Excel

Advanced formulas, data analysis, data cleaning, lookup functions, pivot tables and business reporting using Excel.

📊 Power BI

Data visualization, dashboards, reports, business intelligence and presenting data-driven insights.

📄 Business Requirement Documentation

Requirement gathering, requirement analysis, documentation, process mapping and preparing business requirement documents.

📱 Dashboard & Reporting

Creating professional dashboards, reports and visual presentations for business decision-making.

💼 Case Studies & Project Work

Practical case studies, real-world business problems, project-based learning and industry-oriented assignments.

🧠 Skills & Learning Outcomes

After completing the programme, learners will develop practical skills required for entry-level Business Analyst and data-oriented roles.

  • Business Analysis Fundamentals
  • Understanding of Artificial Intelligence and its business applications
  • Data Analysis and Interpretation
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel
  • SQL and Database Querying
  • Power BI and Data Visualization
  • Business Dashboard Development
  • Business Requirement Documentation
  • Problem Solving and Analytical Thinking
  • Case Study Analysis
  • Project Documentation and Reporting
  • Presentation and Business Communication
  • Practical Training and Project Work

🚀 Career-Oriented Learning

The programme focuses on practical and industry-oriented skills that can support learners in building careers in Business Analysis, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence and related IT/ITES roles.
