Course Structure

Core Learning Modules

📢 Digital Marketing Fundamentals Introduction to digital marketing, digital channels, customer journey, online business models and marketing strategies.
🔎 Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, content optimization, link building and search engine visibility.
📱 Social Media Marketing Social media strategy, content planning, audience engagement, campaign management and social media branding.
📊 Data Analytics & Reporting Digital analytics, campaign performance, KPIs, reports, audience insights and data-driven marketing decisions.
🎯 Online Advertising Digital advertising concepts, campaign planning, audience targeting, ad creatives, performance tracking and optimization.
✍️ Content Marketing Content strategy, copywriting, blogs, visual content, storytelling and digital brand communication.
🤖 Artificial Intelligence for Marketing AI-powered content creation, marketing automation, idea generation, research, productivity and campaign assistance.
🧠 AI Tools & Prompting Practical use of AI tools, prompt writing, AI-assisted content creation and workflow optimization.
📧 Email & Digital Communication Email marketing concepts, campaign planning, audience segmentation and customer communication.
🛒 E-Commerce & Online Business E-commerce fundamentals, online customer acquisition, digital branding and conversion strategies.
📈 Campaign Optimization Campaign monitoring, performance analysis, conversion improvement and marketing optimization.
🚀 Project Work Practical digital marketing projects, campaign planning, reporting and presentation.
